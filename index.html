OUR APPROACH

Respect the source. Show the evidence.

01 / Source-led

Upstream repositories and their licenses remain the authority for implementation details.

02 / Clearly labelled

Verified facts, upstream descriptions and our own observations are distinguished.

03 / Practical

Small, inspectable examples are preferred over claims that cannot be reproduced.
